## About

I am an applied machine learning researcher and engineer 
focused on **adaptive AI systems for specialized domains**. 
My work spans three interconnected research areas:

1. **Continual Learning** — algorithms that enable AI 
   systems to learn new tasks without losing previously 
   acquired knowledge

2. **Large Language Model Adaptation** — parameter-efficient 
   fine-tuning methods for adapting LLMs to specialized 
   domains including medical and multilingual contexts

3. **Trustworthy AI Applications** — misinformation 
   detection, multilingual fraud detection, and medical 
   imaging analysis

I bring 10 years of production software engineering 
experience to this research, with particular emphasis on 
methods that are practical to deploy at scale.

I hold an MS in Computer Science from the University of 
Houston (2023), where I worked on continual learning 
methods in a federally-funded research lab. Currently I 
am an ML/Application Developer at Crane Worldwide 
Logistics.

## Research Interests

- **Continual Learning** — catastrophic forgetting prevention,
  feature-space regularization, replay-based methods
- **LLM Adaptation** — parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA, 
  adapters), domain specialization
- **Medical AI** — medical question answering, image 
  segmentation, healthcare ML deployment
- **Multilingual NLP** — low-resource languages, 
  cross-lingual transfer, transformer models
- **Trustworthy ML** — misinformation detection, 
  fraud detection, robust deployment
