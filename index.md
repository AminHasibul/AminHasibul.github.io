## About

I'm a software engineer with 10 years of experience building distributed backend systems and cloud-native infrastructure, with a growing focus on applied machine learning research alongside my full-time engineering work.

My research spans three interconnected areas:

1. **Continual Learning** — algorithms that let AI systems learn new tasks without losing previously acquired knowledge
2. **LLM Adaptation & Evaluation** — parameter-efficient fine-tuning for specialized domains, and evaluation methodology for vision-language models (hallucination mitigation, grounding-metric auditing)
3. **Trustworthy AI Applications** — misinformation detection, multilingual fraud detection, and medical imaging analysis

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Houston (2023), where I worked on continual learning methods in a federally-funded research lab. I currently work as a backend/distributed systems engineer at Crane Worldwide Logistics, and collaborate independently with a multi-institution research group on applied AI/ML.

## Research Interests

- **Continual Learning** — catastrophic forgetting prevention, feature-space regularization, replay-based methods
- **LLM Adaptation** — parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA, QLoRA), domain specialization
- **Multimodal Evaluation** — vision-language model hallucination, grounding-metric validity, video-QA reasoning
- **Medical AI** — medical question answering, image segmentation, healthcare ML
- **Multilingual NLP** — low-resource languages, cross-lingual transfer, transformer models
- **Trustworthy ML** — misinformation detection, fraud detection, robust deployment
